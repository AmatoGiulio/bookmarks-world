## Edoardo Lunardi / Codrops — Infinite Canvas

The active spatial runtime is adapted from the open-source Infinite Canvas project by Edoardo Lunardi / Codrops:

- Repository: https://github.com/edoardolunardi/infinite-canvas
- Article: https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/01/07/infinite-canvas-building-a-seamless-pan-anywhere-image-space/
- License: MIT

The Bookmarks World implementation preserves the project’s camera/inertia, chunk lifecycle, distance/depth fading, texture-loading and DPR strategy, while replacing its generated/repeating artwork field with stable bookmark identities and product-specific interactions.
